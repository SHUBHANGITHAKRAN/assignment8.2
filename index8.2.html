Key Technical Implementation Details: 8 High-Quality Images (Step 1 & 2): Used Unsplash royalty-free images wrapped inside .gallery-item containers. Auto-Responsive Grid (Step 3):grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(250px, 1fr)); auto-fit automatically calculates how many columns can fit based on available screen space. minmax(250px, 1fr) guarantees each image item is at least 250px wide, but stretches equally (1fr) to fill any extra space. Image Fit & Spacing (Step 4 & 5): width: 100% ensures the image fills its container item width. object-fit: cover preserves proper aspect ratio without squishing or stretching images. gap: 1.5rem maintains clean spacing between grid cards across all screen sizes. Verification (Step 6): Desktop View: Displays 4 columns per row (~1200px width). Tablet View: Automatically reflows into 2–3 columns per row without media queries. Mobile View: Automatically stacks into 1 single column when width drops below ~500px. Recommended Screenshots for Submission: To complete step 6 for your submission: Open the HTML file in your browser at full screen and take a Desktop Screenshot (showing 3–4 columns in a row). Resize your browser window down to mobile width (or open DevTools Ctrl + Shift + I / Cmd + Option + I to simulate iPhone/Mobile view) and take a Mobile Screenshot (showing 1–2 columns).
